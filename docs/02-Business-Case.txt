1. BACKGROUND

   The beauty and cosmetics industry increasingly relies on
   digital platforms to reach customers, promote products and
   build brand trust. Many small shops still rely only on
   physical stores or social media posts, which limits
   discoverability and structured product browsing.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

   The shop currently has no dedicated website. Customers
   cannot easily:
     - Browse products by category
     - View descriptions and indicative prices in one place
     - Access contact details and location quickly
     - Use the shop conveniently on mobile devices

3. PROPOSED SOLUTION

   Build a simple, responsive website prototype that:
     - Introduces the shop and brand
     - Displays products by category with images, names,
       descriptions and prices
     - Provides About and Contact sections
     - Works on desktop, tablet and mobile

4. EXPECTED BENEFITS

   - Improved online presence and professionalism
   - Easier product discovery for customers
   - Central point for contact details and social media
   - Foundation for future e-commerce features
   - Practical portfolio evidence of project management skill

5. COST SUMMARY

   Estimated cost   : LKR 500 (small reserve)
   Actual cost      : LKR 0

   All tools used are free: HTML/CSS/JavaScript, VS Code,
   Git, GitHub, Figma free plan.

6. RISK SUMMARY

   Key risks:
     - Delayed shop information (R01)
     - Unapproved branding and images (R03)
     - Scope expansion (R04)
     - Timeline slippage (R08)

   Full details in docs/08-Risk-Register.txt.

7. RECOMMENDATION

   Proceed with the four-week website prototype project using
   an Agile-inspired approach. Focus on delivering a working,
   tested prototype rather than a full e-commerce platform.
